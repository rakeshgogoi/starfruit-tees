★ Our Story

Born in Bengaluru.
Built for Bharat.

House of Starfruit started with one question: why does fan merchandise have to feel cheap? We set out to build something different — apparel that honours the culture, the teams, and the legends that move us.

Every jersey we make goes through strict quality checks. 220 GSM cotton, zero-fade eco inks, and stitching that lasts. Because you deserve to wear your passion without compromise.

From Bengaluru to every corner of India, we're building a brand rooted in cricket, culture, and community.
